The Role

As a Technical Designer, I serve as the bridge between 2D concept artists and the manufacturing team. My role involves interpreting artistic intent while ensuring the designs are practical for physical production.

This requires understanding material constraints, joint mechanics, paint application requirements, and assembly sequences while maintaining the visual impact of the original concept.

Workflow

1

Concept Analysis

Study the 2D concept to understand proportions, key features, and the intended character/personality of the design.

2

3D Modeling

Build the 3D model with production requirements in mind: wall thickness, parting lines, undercuts, and assembly tolerances.

3

Joint Engineering

Design articulation points that provide good range of motion while maintaining structural integrity and aesthetics.

4

Production Handoff

Prepare final files with part separation, color callouts, and technical documentation for the manufacturing team.
